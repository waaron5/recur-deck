What Is GPS Fleet Tracking and How Does It Work?

GPS fleet tracking is a vehicle monitoring system that uses satellite positioning and cellular networks to report each vehicle's location, speed, and status every 5 to 10 seconds to a centralized dashboard. Businesses use GPS fleet tracking to reduce fuel costs by 10-25%, improve driver safety, monitor compliance with hours-of-service regulations, and optimize route efficiency across fleets of any size.

Hardwired, OBD, Dashcam, and Portable Tracker Hardware

ATv4+ and ATv4-Pro Hardware for Fleet Vehicles and Heavy Equipment

US Fleet Tracking offers five primary GPS tracker models for vehicles, trailers, and equipment: the hardwired ATv4+ ($129) and ATv4-Pro ($199) for fleet vehicles and heavy equipment, the OBD-plug-in QWKv4 ($75) for light-duty vehicles, the driver-behavior TF-V4 ($149), and the IZON dashcam series ($129-$189) with Automatic Driver Assist System (ADAS). Cellular Network Coverage and Update Intervals

5-Second and 10-Second Real-Time Reporting on 4G/LTE

Every USFT device uses 4G/LTE Cat-M1 cellular connectivity and supports user-selectable 5-second or 10-second update intervals. Cat-M1 delivers deep building penetration, low power draw, and broad carrier coverage, which means reliable live tracking for vehicles, trailers, and equipment operating in urban, rural, and indoor environments alike.

Frequently Asked Questions About GPS Fleet Tracking and Management

How Does GPS Fleet Tracking Work?

GPS fleet tracking uses satellite-based positioning (GPS) and cellular networks (4G/LTE Cat-M1) to determine each vehicle's location every 5 to 10 seconds, then displays that data on a live map dashboard. Fleet managers can view routes, speed, stops, and geofence events in real time from any device, with 90 days of historical playback available for reporting and compliance.

What Support Is Included with US Fleet Tracking?

Every US Fleet Tracking subscription includes U.S.-based phone, email, and live chat support from our Oklahoma team, plus an online knowledge base and onboarding assistance. Our support specialists help with device installation, platform setup, geofence configuration, report building, and training drivers and dispatchers, so fleets get live help at every step of the rollout and beyond.

How Do I Get Started with US Fleet Tracking?

Getting started takes three simple steps. Step one is choosing the right GPS tracker for each vehicle or piece of equipment from our AT, QWK, TF, and IZON families. Step two is activating the device on our 4G/LTE Cat-M1 network, which our support team walks you through during a guided onboarding call. Step three is configuring your live map, geofences, alerts, and reports, after which most fleets are seeing real-time location data within minutes.

Why Choose US Fleet Tracking?

Founded in 2005 and based in Edmond, Oklahoma, US Fleet Tracking provides GPS tracking devices with live 5-second and 10-second update intervals — the fastest real-time tracking available without a contract. The platform includes live traffic and weather overlays, geofencing with unlimited zones, and 90-day activity playback, with devices used by Super Bowl security teams and over 100 law enforcement agencies nationwide.

Do You Offer a Free Trial?

Can GPS Fleet Tracking Help With Vehicle Maintenance?

Yes. The US Fleet Tracking platform includes maintenance alerts triggered by mileage or engine hours, allowing fleet managers to schedule oil changes, tire rotations, and inspections automatically. It also tracks idle time and aggressive driving behavior (hard braking, rapid acceleration), which reduces vehicle wear and extends fleet lifespan.

What Features Does the Fleet Tracking Platform Include?
